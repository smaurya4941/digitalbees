Design System Engineering & Behavioral Tokens

TeamBees Corp DS-Spec 2025
1. Motion & Durations

Dropdown transition: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) at 220ms. Stagger child columns by 25ms.

2. Scroll Thresholds

Transparent hero switches to solid #0B1F3A at window.scrollY > 40px with backdrop-filter: blur(16px).

3. Keyboard Accessibility

Key / or Cmd+K triggers full search modal. Escape dismisses active panels. Tab sequence wraps properly.

4. Z-Index Stratification

Header shell: z-50, Mega-menu panels: z-55, Search modal overlay: z-100.
